# GPT-2 Image Prompt Templates for Crowdfunding Plan Decks

## Unified Style Keywords
Use these across all prompts for visual consistency:

```
premium sleep tech, quiet luxury bedroom, cinematic but realistic,
warm low light, soft fabric texture, urban professionals,
high-end wellness product, calm and intimate, not medical,
Oura / Whoop / Eight Sleep brand aesthetic
```

## Universal Avoid List
```
hospital, doctor, lab, brain scan machine, exaggerated glowing brain,
cyberpunk blue-purple neon, sci-fi electric arcs, painful expressions,
product deformation
```

## Base Template
```
Premium lifestyle photography for a high-end [CATEGORY] brand,
[SUBJECT], [SETTING], warm low lighting,
realistic fabric texture, calm and intimate mood,
cinematic composition, shallow depth of field,
editorial product photography, no medical setting,
no hospital, no wires, no sci-fi glow, 16:9
```

## Page-by-Page Prompt Patterns

### Cover / Hero
Subject: modern urban professional wearing sleek dark gray smart sleep mask
Setting: quiet luxury bedroom, soft warm bedside lighting
Mood: calm and peaceful

### Executive Summary
Subject: half-body close-up at bedtime wearing premium sleep mask
Setting: warm bedroom light, linen bedding texture
Mood: calm relaxed expression

### Why Now / Market Judgment
Subject: person lying in bed staring at ceiling OR sitting on edge of bed
Setting: phone screen dimly showing sleep score on nightstand, warm amber light
Mood: quiet frustrated but composed, premium

### Product Positioning
Subject: sleek dark gray smart sleep mask held in hands or on premium nightstand
Setting: minimalist luxury bedroom, soft fabric texture visible
Mood: editorial product still life

### User Personas
Format: 4-panel lifestyle photography grid
Subjects: office at dusk, airport hotel, bedroom with smartwatch, co-working lounge
Mood: warm tones, cinematic editorial, no people or minimal silhouettes

### Reddit Insights
Subject: person lying awake in premium bed with soft wrinkled linen sheets
Setting: warm low light from single bedside lamp
Mood: quiet sleeplessness but composed and beautiful, not distressing

### Comfort Proof (highest priority)
Subject: person sleeping on side, mask fits against pillow without riding up
Setting: another person sleeping in background, soft warm bedroom light
Mood: genuine intimate moment, not studio lighting

### Use Case — Night Sleep
Subject: urban professional returning home, changing into sleepwear, putting on mask
Setting: warm-lit apartment bedroom at night, lived-in luxury interior
Mood: casual authentic moment

### Use Case — Nap Mode
Subject: person reclining on designer sofa or lounge chair wearing mask
Setting: premium office lounge, daytime natural light through large windows
Mood: normalizing restorative rest in professional setting

### Use Case — Jet Lag
Subject: person sleeping in hotel bed wearing mask
Setting: luxury hotel room, city skyline through windows, half-open suitcase
Mood: cosmopolitan calm, travel lifestyle

### App & Data
Subject: smartphone on nightstand showing dark-theme sleep app interface
Setting: mask beside phone, warm morning sunlight through curtains
Mood: clean calm editorial product shot

### Safety & Boundaries
Subject: dark gray mask on wooden nightstand, glass of water, book, lamp
Setting: quiet bedside still life
Mood: safe, calm, peaceful — no people

### Prelaunch Funnel
Subject: person on modern sofa browsing product landing page on smartphone
Setting: warm evening light, casual premium interior
Mood: relaxed lifestyle

## Pages That Do NOT Need Generated Images
- Competitive Landscape → 2x2 matrix infographic
- Page Structure → wireframe mockups
- Perk Strategy → pricing cards + product renderings
- Timeline & Budget → Gantt chart + bar charts
- Trust & Team → institutional logos + typography (NEVER fake portraits)
- Marketing Mix → ecosystem map infographic
