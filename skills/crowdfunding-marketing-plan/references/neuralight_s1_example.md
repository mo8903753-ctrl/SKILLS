## Key Decisions Made

### Tone Calibration (3 rounds of iteration)
- Round 1: English-first, too translated-from-English
- Round 2: Chinese-first but too casual ("该干点实际的了" rejected)
- Round 3 (final): Professional but natural agency Chinese — sounds like a real MadSpace director in a meeting

### Claim Softening
- "全球首款" → "a new generation of" (Muse/Elemind/Somnee exist)
- "不会压眼睛/打扰伴侣/断信号" → "设计目标是…建议上线前验证"
- "入睡时间缩短" → "（需品牌方提供测试数据支撑）"
- FAQ absolute answers → "需品牌方确认" / "建议测试阶段验证"

### KPI Corrections
- ROAS base: $180K ads (not $208K total budget)
- Deposit KPI: 800-1,200 (not 200-350) for $1M project
- Bundle math: explicit table with 15-18% bundle mix assumption
