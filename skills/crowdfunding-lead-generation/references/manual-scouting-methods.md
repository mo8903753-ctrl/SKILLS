# Manual Scouting Methods — Chinese Hardware Brands for KS/IGG

> These are the user's own detailed methods for manual lead sourcing. Automation (Kicktraq + 36kr + HN) covers partial data collection, but the judgment, contact-finding, and outreach steps remain manual.

---

## Channel 1: KS/IGG "Naked" Chinese Projects (Gold Mine, Highest ROI)

### Where to find them

- **KS Discover**: Filter by relevant categories (Design & Tech), sort by Newest / End Date Soon. Location filter can help. Focus on **Coming Soon / Prelaunch** pages — projects that haven't launched yet are the best intervention point (still salvageable).
- **Indiegogo Explore**: Same approach. Check **InDemand** section for post-campaign projects.
- **Kicktraq**: Check daily funding curves. A flat curve = "doing poorly" = opportunity. Look for projects where the curve plateaus early.

### How to identify Chinese teams

- Team location reveals Shenzhen/Guangdong/Hangzhou/Shanghai/Beijing
- Chinglish copywriting style
- Typical categories: 3C, outdoor gear, EDC
- Chinese-language comments in the comment section
- Founder names that sound Chinese

### How to judge "bad = good opportunity"

The worse the project is performing, the easier to pitch your services:

- Progress bar stuck (way below goal with little time left)
- Didn't get "Projects We Love" badge
- Poor page quality: rough renderings, no video, KS story poorly told
- Weak first 48 hours = didn't build a pre-launch email list (this is exactly what MadSpace excels at)
- Complaints in comments (shipping, communication issues)

### How to find the people

1. Project page → official website / social media links → LinkedIn to find founders
2. FAQ / Risks section → reveals company name → 企查查 (Qichacha) reverse lookup
3. Search product name on 小红书 (Xiaohongshu) / 微博 (Weibo) / 抖音 (Douyin) → many teams have Chinese-language accounts → DM them
4. **Priority: WeChat**. Chinese teams close fastest on WeChat.

### Outreach angle (critical)

**NOT** "Do you want to cooperate?" 

**INSTEAD**: "I looked at your XX project and noticed several specific issues (pre-launch list too small / landing page capture rate low / ad ROI weak). We helped YY in the same category reach $Z. Here's the case study."

Give value → give benchmark → give evidence.

### Weekly rhythm

- Fixed category checklist
- Scan N new live projects + N prelaunch + N poor-performing projects weekly
- Log everything in a tracking table

## Channel 2: Recently Funded Hardware Companies

### Where to find them

- **IT Juzi (IT桔子)**: Filter by "智能硬件/消费电子/IoT/可穿戴" (smart hardware/consumer electronics/IoT/wearables), last 1-2 weeks
- **36kr funding news**
- **烯牛数据 (Xiniu Data)**, **企名片 (Qimingpian)**

### How to judge

- Angel to Series A stage (early, has money, wants to go global but doesn't have mature overseas team yet)
- Category fit
- Has physical product
- Founder has mentioned global markets

### Outreach

- Funding news → company name → 企查查/LinkedIn/脉脉 to find founder or marketing lead
- Angle: "Congrats on the funding. If you have overseas plans, crowdfunding is the lowest-cost overseas launch + user validation. We've done YY for similar companies."

## Channel 3: 1688 / Xiaohongshu / Douyin Emerging Original Hardware Brands

### Where + signals

- **1688**: Search for original-design products (自主设计/专利, NOT public-mold white-label). Factory-to-brand signals = starting to create logo, brand story, design patents.
- **Xiaohongshu / Douyin**: Search for novel hardware products. Find brand owners / factory owners running their own accounts. Look for emerging products with originality.
- **Bonus signal**: Already selling well domestically + original design/tech + overseas market still untouched.

### How to judge

- High originality
- Has domestic sales + good reviews
- Zero overseas presence
- Founder has brand ambition (not just factory mentality)

### Outreach

- DM on Xiaohongshu / Douyin
- Business WeChat from profile
- On 1688, find the brand decision-maker (not customer service)
- Angle: "Your XX is already proven domestically. Overseas is a blank slate. Crowdfunding = low-cost market test + brand building + first batch of overseas users..."

### ⚠️ Warning

This channel has the lowest lead temperature (they haven't even thought about going overseas yet). Long nurture cycle. Put them in a long-term cultivation pool, don't expect quick conversions.
