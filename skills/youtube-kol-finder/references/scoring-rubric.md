# Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to rank YouTube KOL candidates for Western tech crowdfunding projects.

## Score Bands

- `Yes`: 75-100 points. Prioritize outreach.
- `Backup`: 60-74 points. Keep for budget fallback, category expansion, or secondary waves.
- `No`: below 60 points, or any hard rejection.

## 100-Point Score

| Factor | Points | What to Check |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience and category fit | 25 | Strong overlap with the product category, early adopters, makers, tech buyers, or relevant niche users. |
| Recent performance | 20 | Recent average views on relevant long-form videos, stability across the last 5-10 videos, and no obvious view collapse. |
| Crowdfunding/conversion readiness | 15 | Prior Kickstarter/Indiegogo/launch coverage, product review depth, buying-intent audience, and ability to explain new hardware. |
| Engagement quality | 15 | Real comments, technical questions, purchase intent, creator replies, and low spam. |
| Cost efficiency | 15 | Fee versus recent average views, quote flexibility, product-exchange potential, and expected CPM. |
| Outreach/logistics fit | 10 | Public contact availability, English content, target-market fit, sample feasibility, and willingness to coordinate timing. |

## Cost Efficiency Guide

Use CPM only when both fee and recent average views are available:

`CPM = fee / average views * 1000`

Interpret by category and channel quality:

- Strong: low CPM plus strong audience fit and real engagement.
- Workable: medium CPM with excellent niche relevance or crowdfunding history.
- Risky: high CPM without proof of conversion, unstable views, or weak category fit.
- Product exchange can be high priority if the channel has strong fit, authentic comments, and realistic review depth.

Do not rank purely by subscriber count. A smaller channel with stable niche views and strong comments can outrank a large general tech channel.

## Hard Rejections

Mark as `No` when any of these apply:

- Only covers phone news or generic tech news with no product review depth.
- Refuses crowdfunding or launch-stage products.
- Quote is clearly inflated for recent average views.
- Recent long-form views are sharply declining.
- Audience quality looks suspicious or comment sections are mostly spam.
- Channel is Shorts-only when the campaign needs long-form review.
- Content is too broad to identify likely buyers.
- Channel is not English-language, unless the user requested that market.
- Contact path is unavailable and no practical outreach route can be found.

## Notes Style

Write concise, operational notes:

- Good: `Yes - US maker audience, 40K avg long-form views, prior Kickstarter reviews, product-exchange possible.`
- Good: `Backup - strong category fit but fee unknown; verify rate and prelaunch timing.`
- Good: `No - phone news only, weak review depth, recent videos below 5K views.`
- Avoid vague notes such as `looks good` or `not suitable`.
