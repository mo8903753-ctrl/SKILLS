# CP Series: Scaling-First Pricing Methodology

Learned from the CP Series feasibility study. Applicable when the brand client's
product has a fixed entry price that IS the main volume driver, and bundles are
secondary.

## Core Strategic Choice

When the brand's price is locked (e.g., $149.99), the proposal must answer:
**scale-first or AOV-first?**

- **Scale-first**: Entry price is THE volume driver. $500K requires 3,000+ backers.
  Bundles and add-ons lift AOV but are NOT the primary lever. KPI anchors on
  backer count.
- **AOV-first**: Bundles are the primary lever. AOV $220-315 drives the goal with
  1,590-2,300 backers. This is Segai's model (~$315 avg pledge).

CP chose scale-first. This means:
- KPI: backers 2,000-2,800 (not 1,590)
- AOV: $180-250 (not $220-315)
- Budget: same 17.96% formula, but leads targets scale proportionally
- Timeline: KOL寄送 ≥4 weeks before launch, 预热 ≥3 weeks

## Pricing Cards That Show Both Models

When the client is debating scale vs AOV, show 4 scenarios:

| Scenario | AOV | Backers needed | Message |
|----------|-----|----------------|---------|
| Pure entry | $150 | ~3,334 | Worst case |
| Light bundle | $200 | ~2,500 | Realistic |
| Heavy bundle | $250 | ~2,000 | Optimistic |
| Segai-style | $315 | ~1,590 | Competitor benchmark |

Never label the Segai row as "Segai实际" — it implies Segai's numbers ARE $315
× 1,590, which is wrong. Segai did ~$460K / 1,400+ backers / ~$315-325 avg pledge.
Label it "对标 Segai 客单水平" or "Segai 客单优先路线（对照）".

## Tier Design for Scale-First

```
Super Early Bird  $139-149  限量首发，拉首日冲量
Standard          $149.99   客户定价，入门主力档 ← MOST ORDERS HERE
Hygiene Bundle    $199-219  杯 + 底座，主推套餐
Couple Bundle     $279-299  双设备 + 联动，核心AOV驱动
Add-ons           $19-49    替换内胆、支架、配件
```

No "Early Bird Kit" between SEB and Standard — the gap is too small to matter.

## KPI Auto-Consistency Check

When AOV changes, backer count MUST recalculate:
- $500K ÷ $150 = 3,334 backers
- $500K ÷ $200 = 2,500 backers
- $500K ÷ $250 = 2,000 backers
- $500K ÷ $315 = 1,590 backers

The footnote must explain the relationship:
"按 $180–250 AOV 测算，$500K 约需 2,000–2,800 backers · 综合AOV随套餐结构浮动，套餐设计是关键变量"

## Competitive Claim Softening

When the product claims superiority over a competitor, verify:
1. Does the competitor actually lack the feature, or do they have a different approach?
2. Is the competitor's "lack" documented or assumed?

Before: "AI伴侣生态：Segai没有"
After: "AI伴侣生态落地度：Segai已提出开放平台/虚拟亲密生态愿景；CP应强调'已可演示'的Spine 2D角色伴侣、语音、声线克隆与AI图片/视频能力"

Before: "免脚本同步直接打击Segai"
After: "免脚本同步已被验证 — Segai已证明No scripts needed是强用户利益；CP不把它当差异点，而是在同等AI同步基础上强化体感与AI伴侣体验"

The rule: if the competitor has demonstrated/announced something in the same
direction, don't claim they lack it. Claim you execute it better or differently.

## Competitor Price Band Accuracy

Never use rounded or approximate competitor prices without qualification:

| Competitor | Bad | Good |
|-----------|-----|------|
| The Handy | ~$199-279 | ~$169起；Handy 2/Pro更高 |
| Lovense | ~$199-399 | ~$99-250常见成交价；Solace Pro MSRP可到~$399 |
| Kiiroo Keon | ~$229 | ~$199-229 |
| Segai | ~$315 avg. | ~$315-325 avg pledge（非零售价） |

For KS competitors, distinguish "avg pledge" from "retail price" — they're
different numbers and confusing them misleads the client.

## Meta/Google Budget Split

Default: Meta 70% + Google 30% (not 60:40). This was user-corrected during CP
proposal iteration.

## Public Goal Strategy

Always recommend: public KS goal set LOW ($15-30K for a $500K internal target),
denominated in the campaign currency (HKD for Segai: HK$100K ≈ US$12.8K).
Purpose: rapid "funded" badge → momentum → algorithm boost.
